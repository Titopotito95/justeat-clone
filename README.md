## 🍽 What is JustEat Clone?  
The **[JustEat Clone](https://sangvish.com/justeat-clone/)** is a ready-made, white-label food delivery app solution that mirrors the **core functionality** of the JustEat platform. It empowers **entrepreneurs, startups, and restaurant aggregators** to quickly launch their on-demand food ordering business with:

- 📱 Native Android & iOS mobile apps  
- 💻 Fully responsive web panels  
- 🖥 Centralized admin dashboard for complete control  
- 💰 Flexible revenue models — commission-based & subscription  

---

## 🔄 How it Works  
1. **Customer** browses restaurants and places an order.  
2. **Restaurant** confirms and prepares the order.  
3. **Delivery Partner** picks up and delivers in real time.  
4. **Admin** monitors and manages all platform activities.
